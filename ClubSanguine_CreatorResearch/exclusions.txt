# Research guardrails / exclusions for Club Sanguine creator research
#
# These are sensible defaults to keep research focused and high-signal.
# They are guardrails, not absolute bans — if a creator is an exceptional
# fit despite matching one of these, note the exception and reasoning
# rather than silently excluding them.

- Inactive creators (no posts/streams in a long time), unless they are an exceptional fit
- Pure esports channels (competitive-only, no relevant gameplay coverage)
- Minecraft-only creators
- Fortnite-only creators
- Generic gaming news channels with no gameplay content
- Clip/reupload channels (no original commentary or content)
- Channels primarily focused on unrelated AAA walkthroughs (e.g. open-world blockbusters, sports titles, shooters) with no indie/horror/narrative coverage
- Creators with no meaningful evidence of relevant game coverage
- Non-English creators, for this initial research phase (may be revisited in a later phase)
- Extremely large creators/celebrities, unless they show exceptional direct fit with the seed games or genre
