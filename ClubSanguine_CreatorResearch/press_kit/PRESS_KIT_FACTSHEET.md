## Short description
Club Sanguine is a darkly comic first-person deduction and nightclub-management game. Inspect suspicious clubgoers with garlic, silver, holy water and other tools, expose impostors, and decide who gets admitted to your vampire haven — and who goes down the trapdoor.

## Longer description
Welcome to Club Sanguine, a rundown vampire nightclub with a strict door policy and a staff that might be more dangerous than the customers. Each night you wake up in your coffin and prepare the club for business. Once the doors open, the queue fills with vampires, their loyal thralls, humans, zombies, demons, werewolves and other hopeful impostors.

A good bouncer needs more than a guest list. Inspect people with garlic, silver, UV light, holy water and other suspicious tools. Learn their tells, catch their lies and weigh the consequences of every decision. Send the legitimate undead to the dance floor, redirect unwanted guests to the blood room or banishment, and reach for the trapdoor lever when diplomacy fails.

Between shifts, buy equipment, improve the club and manage the chaos with the help of Frank, your not-so-subtle security guard, a highly motivated mummy cleaner and the rest of your supernatural crew. Keep the blood flowing, the wrong people out and the vampire world hidden. Nosferatu is coming to inspect your progress.

The free PC demo is available now on Steam.
