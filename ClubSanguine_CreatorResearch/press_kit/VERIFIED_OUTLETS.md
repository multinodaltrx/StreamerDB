# Verified intake routes — checked 1 Nov 2026

- **Alpha Beta Gamer:** Admin@alphabetagamer.com — specifically invites free in-development games/demos; https://www.alphabetagamer.com/contact-us/
- **Indie Games Plus:** editors@indiegamesplus.com — directly asks developers to share games; https://indiegamesplus.com/contact/
- **GamesRadar+:** news@gamesradar.com — official *news tips* mailbox, not freelance article pitch mailbox; https://www.gamesradar.com/gamesradar-pitching-guidelines-how-to-pitch/
- **PC Gamer:** tips@pcgamer.com — official *news tips* inbox, may not be the ideal cold indie-game demo pitch; https://www.pcgamer.com/tips/
- **IGN:** use 'Editorial News Tips & Content' on https://ign-corp.squarespace.com/contact . Do NOT use IGN sales/advertising or freelance contributor pitch routes.
- **GamingOnLinux:** https://www.gamingonlinux.com/contact-us/ — contact/tip form; verify Linux support BEFORE pitching because Club Sanguine is currently Windows-only. Lower priority unless they cover Proton playability after actual testing.
- **Wishlist or Not:** press@wishlistornot.com — explicitly reviews PC indie demos and event builds; https://wishlistornot.com/press/

Important: DreadXP's `pitches@dreadxp.com` is for pitching a game to a publisher, NOT a general editorial submission mailbox, and DreadXP has strict generative-AI restrictions. Do not treat that as a news outlet.

## Follow-ups
- Research a specific journalist's recent coverage of horror-comedy / deduction / first-person job-sim games before an individual pitch.
- Verify named addresses immediately before sending. Never assume historical lists are current.
- Begin with highly relevant indie and demo-review channels, then scale to bigger outlets.
